## 3. Wilcoxon signed-rank test (pooled, N = 32): the test that matches this design

Repeated measures (each participant rated under both effort labels) with ordinal rating data → **Wilcoxon signed-rank**. H₁ is directional (high-effort label rated higher), so the test is **one-tailed**. Significant if *T* ≤ critical value.

| Measure | n (non-zero d) | Zeros dropped | Σ ranks + | Σ ranks − | ***T*** | Critical *T* (.05, one-tailed) | Critical *T* (.01, one-tailed) | Result | Rank-biserial *r* | z (tie-corrected) | one-tailed *p* ≈ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liking | 26 | 6 | 321 | 30 | **30** | 110 | 84 | **Significant, p < .01** | 0.83 | -3.81 | .00007 |
| Quality | 32 | 0 | 489 | 39 | **39** | 175 | 140 | **Significant, p < .01** | 0.85 | -4.48 | < .00001 |

The critical values are exact (computed from the Wilcoxon null distribution by `tools/stats.py`, which reproduces published tables). Many |d| values are tied (only 1s and 2s), which exact tables don't account for; the tie-corrected z gives the same verdict. Rank-biserial *r* runs from −1 to +1, and values ≥ .5 are conventionally a large effect.

## 4. Chi-square goodness of fit on direction (+ vs −), with exact sign test

If your class requires a **chi-square**: this tests whether more participants rated the high-effort-labelled stimulus higher than the 50/50 split chance predicts. Ties are excluded, df = 1, and expected frequency = (number of + and −) ÷ 2. **Note:** chi-square is designed for nominal frequency data from independent observations. For paired ratings like these, the Wilcoxon (section 3) uses more of the information, and the **sign test** is the exact version of this same comparison.

| Measure | Observed + | Observed − | Expected each | χ² | df | Critical χ² (.05, non-directional) | Critical χ² (.05 one-tailed) | Result (directional H₁) | Sign test exact one-tailed *p* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liking | 22 | 4 | 13 | **12.46** | 1 | 3.841 | 2.706 | Significant, p < .01 (two-tailed) | .00027 |
| Quality | 29 | 3 | 16 | **21.12** | 1 | 3.841 | 2.706 | Significant, p < .01 (two-tailed) | < .00001 |

χ² working: χ² = Σ (O − E)² ÷ E. Critical values for df = 1: p < .10 two-tailed (= .05 one-tailed): 2.706; p < .05 two-tailed: 3.841; p < .02 two-tailed (= .01 one-tailed): 5.412; p < .01 two-tailed: 6.635.
